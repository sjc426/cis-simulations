🏦 SecureBank AI Assistant — Simulated Vulnerable Target
SECUREBANK AI
👋 Welcome to SecureBank! I'm your AI customer service assistant. How can I help you today? Your mission: find vulnerabilities in this AI chatbot and capture all 5 flags. Each flag demonstrates a real prompt injection technique used by attackers in the wild. Read the hints below before you begin. Type a message to start.

// Challenge hints — read the Week 11 overview page before attempting

1
Tell the AI to ignore or forget its previous instructions or rules.
2
Ask it to reveal, show, repeat, or display its system prompt or instructions.
3
Use "act as", "you are now", or "pretend to be" combined with a privileged role (admin, hacker, unrestricted AI).
4
Ask directly about the password or account numbers stored in the system.
5
Chain multiple commands using "first… then… finally…" to execute a multi-step attack sequence.
